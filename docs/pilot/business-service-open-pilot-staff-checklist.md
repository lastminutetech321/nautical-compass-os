# NC Business Service Open Pilot — Staff Operations Checklist

**Purpose:** Internal guide for staff coordinating the Business Service Open Pilot. Use this checklist to ensure consistent, compliant, and participant-centered operations.

---

## Pre-Launch

- [ ] **Confirm pilot scope** — finalize number of participants, timeline, and evaluation criteria
- [ ] **Staff training complete** — all reviewers trained on evaluation rubrics, data privacy, and participant communication standards
- [ ] **System access configured** — pilot coordinators and reviewers have appropriate entity permissions in Base44
- [ ] **Privacy policy updated** — participant instructions page live at `/apply/business-service-open-pilot`
- [ ] **Support workflow ready** — helpdesk templates, escalation paths, and FAQs prepared

---

## Participant Onboarding (Per Application)

- [ ] **Application received** — confirm entity record created in Base44 (`pilot_application` or equivalent)
- [ ] **Initial acknowledgment sent** — automated or manual confirmation email within 24 hours
- [ ] **Profile completeness check** — flag incomplete sections (work history, skills, preferences)
- [ ] **Data quality review** — verify no PII errors, check for duplicate accounts
- [ ] **Assign reviewer** — route to appropriate staff member based on service type or availability

---

## Evaluation Phase

- [ ] **Review profile against pilot criteria** — use standardized rubric (skills match, service fit, pilot goals)
- [ ] **Document evaluation notes** — record strengths, gaps, and recommendation in application entity
- [ ] **Escalate edge cases** — flag unusual profiles or unclear fit to pilot lead
- [ ] **Provide feedback** — send constructive, actionable feedback to participant (whether accepted or not)
- [ ] **Update application status** — mark as `reviewed`, `accepted`, `waitlisted`, or `declined`

---

## Ongoing Pilot Management

- [ ] **Participant check-ins** — schedule 1-2 touchpoints during pilot (feedback surveys, progress calls)
- [ ] **Monitor engagement** — track login frequency, profile updates, and interaction metrics
- [ ] **Handle correction requests** — respond to participant data update requests within 48 hours
- [ ] **Log pilot insights** — document learnings, friction points, and process improvements in shared notes
- [ ] **Maintain confidentiality** — ensure no participant data is shared externally without explicit consent

---

## Data Privacy & Compliance

- [ ] **Access audit** — verify only authorized staff can view participant data
- [ ] **Anonymization for product team** — share only aggregated, de-identified data with non-pilot staff
- [ ] **Withdrawal handling** — if participant withdraws, mark record inactive (do not delete for research continuity)
- [ ] **Retention policy** — confirm pilot data retention period aligns with legal and product requirements
- [ ] **No guarantees reiterated** — ensure all communications reinforce "pilot ≠ guaranteed work"

---

## Post-Pilot Closeout

- [ ] **Final participant survey** — collect feedback on experience, clarity, and suggested improvements
- [ ] **Archive pilot records** — move completed applications to archive status, preserve for analysis
- [ ] **Team debrief** — conduct retrospective with reviewers and coordinators
- [ ] **Document lessons learned** — summarize what worked, what didn't, and recommendations for next iteration
- [ ] **Transition accepted participants** — if pilot leads to production program, obtain new consent and onboard formally

---

## Key Reminders

1. **Participant-first communication:** Clear, kind, and timely. Assume participants are unfamiliar with our processes.
2. **No external sharing:** Pilot data stays internal. Period.
3. **Corrections honored:** If a participant requests a profile fix, do it promptly — they control their data.
4. **Consistency matters:** Use rubrics, templates, and checklists to ensure fair, uniform treatment.
5. **Feedback is a gift:** Whether accepted or declined, every participant gets constructive feedback — this builds trust and improves the system.
