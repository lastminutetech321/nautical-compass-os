## Coordinator Rails Reference

### 1. Dispatch System
**Purpose:** Assign jobs to crew members.  
**Key pages:**
- `/dispatch/jobs` — incoming job requests
- `/dispatch/assign` — crew matching interface
- `/dispatch/status` — active job tracking

**Common friction points (to watch for):**
- "How do I know who's available?"
- "Can I assign multiple crew to one job?"
- "What if someone cancels last-minute?"

### 2. Workforce Hub
**Purpose:** Manage crew roster, onboarding, credentials.  
**Key pages:**
- `/workforce/crew` — crew directory
- `/workforce/onboarding` — new crew intake
- `/workforce/credentials` — certifications, background checks

**Common friction points:**
- "Where do I upload crew documents?"
- "How do I mark someone as inactive?"

### 3. Booking Engine
**Purpose:** Client scheduling, availability calendar.  
**Key pages:**
- `/booking/calendar` — availability grid
- `/booking/requests` — incoming client requests
- `/booking/confirm` — finalize bookings

**Common friction points:**
- "Can I block off dates for a crew member?"
- "How do I handle double-bookings?"

### 4. Scheduling Tools
**Purpose:** Coordinate crew and client calendars.  
**Key pages:**
- `/schedule/overview` — weekly/monthly views
- `/schedule/conflicts` — overlap detection

**Common friction points:**
- "How do I sync with external calendars (Google, Outlook)?"
- "Can I get SMS reminders for crew?"

### 5. Contracts
**Purpose:** Manage agreements, e-signatures.  
**Key pages:**
- `/contracts/templates` — standard agreements
- `/contracts/pending` — awaiting signature
- `/contracts/signed` — completed docs

**Common friction points:**
- "How do I customize a contract template?"
- "What if a client wants paper signature?"

## Proactive Assistance Triggers

**System will auto-surface guidance when:**

1. **Brian opens Dispatch for first time** → Show "5-min quickstart video"
2. **Brian hasn't assigned a job in 48 hours after first login** → Nudge: "Ready to try your first dispatch?"
3. **Brian opens Workforce Hub but doesn't add crew** → Tooltip: "Click 'Add Crew' to start"
4. **Brian clicks 'Contracts' 3+ times without sending one** → Pop-up: "Need help with your first contract? Here's a walkthrough."
5. **Brian logs in daily but skips Booking Engine** → Email: "Don't forget to check client booking requests"
